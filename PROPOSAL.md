# Freelance Proposal

## English (Upwork) — about 130 words
Hi! I build **App Radar**, a live competitor intelligence dashboard for mobile apps.

You type your app's name and your competitors' names, and the platform pulls their newest Google Play reviews automatically. A machine learning model flags **critical complaints** (crashes, payment and login failures) and is tuned to miss as few of them as possible. You get clear comparison cards and charts, a live tool to score any new review, and an **AI executive report** that lists competitors' weak points and your top development priorities.

Everything runs in the browser, with no installation, and works on mobile. I can customise it for your app, your market (Arabic or English) and your team's alert rules, and deliver it as a monthly monitoring service.
